## Enquiry Email — "Site visit app"

**From:** Priya Nair, Operations Manager, Harbour Facilities Services
**Date:** 14 March 2026

Hi,

We manage cleaning and maintenance contracts for about 40 commercial buildings. Right now our
supervisors record site visits on paper checklists, then someone types them into a spreadsheet at
the end of the week. We lose forms, and clients keep asking for proof that a visit happened.

We would like a simple app where a supervisor can open the checklist for a building, tick the items,
add a photo or two, and submit. Office staff should be able to see submitted visits. Nothing fancy
for now — we just want to stop using paper and get something in front of a few supervisors to see
if they will actually use it.

We have not built anything like this before, so there is no existing system. We do not have designs.

Supervisors mostly use their own Android phones. Some of the buildings are basements with very poor
signal, which is one reason the paper forms have survived this long.

Could you let us know what you would need from us to move forward?

Thanks,
Priya
